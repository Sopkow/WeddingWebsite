Our story

We met in Victoria, BC on October 25, 2019, over tacos. It was supposed to be a get-to-know-each-other dinner. A few days later we turned it into a camping trip.

The plan was Bamfield. The reality was hours of Vancouver Island back roads, and we figured out too late that we’d forgotten most of the food and all the bedding we needed. Somehow we made a good team anyway. A kind older couple spotted us, took us across the channel by boat, and gave us a campsite on their private beach, looking out over the Pacific.

That weekend kicked everything off. Before long we’d bought our first home together, a live-aboard sailboat named Nyala, while Bran built his software company in downtown Victoria. Then Ava went after her dream of becoming a pilot in New Brunswick, and Bran followed soon after.

He asked, and she said yes. The proposal happened inside a tunnel of a million lights, with our dog right at our feet and a very surprised Ava.

There were happy tears, a long kiss, and a ring held up to the nearest camera. Then we sat in a giant red chair with Duke and tried to stop grinning.

Now we’re planning the party we actually want: a mountain weekend with the people we love, a long potluck table, and a lot of games.
